# Volatco Box Mechanical Specification

## 1) Reference geometry

- Bare board size (from Volatco docs): **60 x 40 x 15 mm**
- ESD protective wrap: **3 mm bubble wrap per side**
- Wrapped board envelope: **66 x 46 x 21 mm**
- Assembly clearance allowance: **+2 mm each axis**
- Final internal box size target: **68 x 48 x 23 mm**

## 2) Box style and panel construction

- Style: **Straight-line folding carton** (reverse tuck top + friction bottom)
- Glue seam: **12 mm** side glue flap (PVA or hot-melt compatible)
- Main panels:
  - Front/back: **68 x 48 mm**
  - Side panels: **23 x 48 mm**
- Closing flaps:
  - Top flap depth: **25 mm**
  - Bottom flap depth: **25 mm**

## 3) Recommended substrate (paperboard)

Optimal target for this small electronics carton:

- Material: **SBS C1S folding boxboard** (Solid Bleached Sulfate, coated one side)
- Caliper (thickness): **0.50 mm** nominal (approx. 20 pt)
- Basis weight range: **350-400 gsm**

Why this is optimal for this size:

- Gives enough bending stiffness to avoid panel bowing on a 68 mm span.
- Holds score lines cleanly for repeated opening/closing.
- Maintains good print quality for saturated branding on front/top faces.
- Prevents over-bulk that would make 23 mm side panels difficult to fold accurately.

Alternative grades:

- Lightweight option: **0.40 mm (16 pt), ~300-325 gsm** (lower cost, less crush resistance)
- Heavy-duty option: **0.60 mm (24 pt), ~420-460 gsm** (higher rigidity, may require wider creases)

## 4) Mechanical performance targets

- Carton should remain closed under normal handling with no tape.
- No visible panel crease whitening on first fold.
- No side wall buckling when lightly squeezed by hand.
- Minimum top-panel load target (static): **1.5 kg** without permanent deformation.

## 5) Score, crease, and tolerance guidance

- Die-cut tolerance: **+/-0.20 mm**
- Score-to-score tolerance: **+/-0.15 mm**
- Recommended crease rule setup for 0.50 mm SBS:
  - Crease width target: **1.0-1.2 mm**
  - Moderate score depth to avoid fiber cracking
- Internal dimension tolerance after folding: **+/-0.5 mm**

## 6) ESD and protection notes

- Use **anti-static pink bubble wrap** or **metallized ESD bubble** at 3 mm thickness.
- Bubble wrap should fully cover PCB edges/corners before insertion.
- If higher ESD sensitivity is expected, add an **ESD shielding bag** around the wrapped board; keep same carton dimensions only if bag film is <=0.2 mm total added thickness.

## 7) Print and finish recommendations

- Exterior print: CMYK + optional spot color for Cartheur mint accent `#20F8A8`.
- Finish: **Matte aqueous coating** (preferred) or matte varnish.
- Avoid heavy lamination on score lines to reduce crack risk.
- Keep critical text/marks at least **3 mm** inside cut edges (safe zone).

## 8) Environmental and logistics

- Preferred board spec: FSC-certified SBS where available.
- Storage: 40-60% RH to maintain fold quality.
- Flat-pack shipping orientation: stack with grain direction parallel to primary vertical folds for cleaner erection.

## 9) Branding palette standard

- Cartheur primary dark: **#101010**
- Cartheur accent mint: **#20F8A8**
- Apply this palette across mockups, dieline, and 3D render for visual consistency.

## 10) Summary recommendation

For this Volatco package size and branding goal, use:

- **SBS C1S, 0.50 mm, 350-400 gsm**, reverse tuck top/friction bottom style, 68 x 48 x 23 mm internal dimensions, with 3 mm anti-static bubble wrap protection.
